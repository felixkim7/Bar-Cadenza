# Confirmed art direction

User decision dated 2026-11-26. This supersedes the exploratory Art Deco/pixel/visual-novel alternatives and emerald-led palette. Written rules govern interpretation of reference images.

## Components
| Component | Direction |
|---|---|
| Character portraits/figures | Flat-color Western cartoon; clean contours, shape-based shadows, expressive poses |
| Interactive props | Flat-color illustration matching characters; crisp silhouettes and legible materials |
| Environment | Classic 2D animation background painting with limited Art Deco editorial influence |
| Foreground UI | Flat, clean geometry; highest contrast where feedback is needed |
| Rendering | 2D raster, layered for selective movement and interaction |
| Period/mood | 1920s–1930s speakeasy; intimate, theatrical, sophisticated, lived-in |

## Palette
Primary: oxblood, burgundy, navy blue, charcoal, supporting dark wood tones. Highlights: aged brass and warm amber. Emerald appears sparingly and never leads the room.

| Supplied color | Exact value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Champagne | #EACEAA | Recipe pages and handwritten orders |
| Honey Garlic | #85431E | Walnut counter and bottle shelves |
| Whiskey Sour | #D39858 | Lamp glow and selected tools |
| Burnt Coffee | #34150F | Booth upholstery and menu panels |
| Balsamico | #150C0C | Deep shadows and printed lettering |

Exact added colors are open until the visual gate. Use named roles; do not label an invented hex as approved. Skin tones, hair, clothing and ingredients may vary naturally while remaining harmonized.

## Hierarchy
1. Interactables read immediately at normal gameplay size: bottles, jiggers, tools, glasses, active ingredient states.
2. Customer expressions/body language carry the emotional focus during conversation.
3. Environment supplies atmosphere without competing with choices.
4. Warm light binds the layers: painterly room; mostly flat characters and props with restrained amber rim light/simple shadow.
5. Consistency beats individual illustration quality: palette, contour, perspective, scale, and light direction agree.

## Layer treatment
| Layer | Edges | Color/shading | Texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Character | Confident dark burgundy/charcoal contour, varied weight | Base + 1–2 hard-edged shadow/highlight shapes | Minimal; restrained fabric only |
| Prop | Precise contour consistent with characters | Base + simplified material highlights | Minimal; simplified labels |
| Background | Softer distant edges; selected architectural lines | Painted warm/cool light and subtle variation | Controlled wood/fabric/wall grain |
| UI | Clean flat geometry | Strong actionable contrast | None or barely perceptible |

## Characters
Recognizably adult, attractive but not hyper-realistic, expressive without chibi proportions. Natural yet slightly stylized head/body ratios, softened facial construction, clear brows/mouths and gestures. Maintain contour weight at displayed size. Shadow boundaries may be softly feathered, but do not airbrush skin. Avoid painterly face texture.

Differentiate by silhouette, posture, costume, hair, accessories, color blocking and personality, not just face. Broad adult ages, appearances, genders and social roles. Do not make everyone equally glamorous or identically dressed. Human fixtures do not settle O010 about potential anthropomorphic patrons.

## Do / avoid
Do: clean shapes, controlled line weights, strong silhouettes, limited shading steps, unified warm highlights, coherent perspective, readable negative space.
Avoid: photorealistic bottles beside cartoon sprites, glossy gradients, heavy anime rendering, highly textured faces, strong distant outlines, neon saturation, arbitrary style shifts, emerald-dominated rooms.
