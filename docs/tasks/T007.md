    - Adding to a served or discarded glass starts a new drink with a new ID.
  - **Routing** (`ui/input_router.py`), in priority order:
    1. A modal takes all keys, but IME commits still go into the draft.
    2. A captured pointer action: a press on a hold control captures the pointer. Releasing anywhere ends the action, and so do focus loss and minimize.
    3. Keyboard focus. While the chat field has focus, every key goes to it, so letters, Space and Enter never trigger a control. The mouse still works the buttons, so the player can type and mix at once. Tab moves focus between the field and the buttons. Leaving the field commits the IME preview into the draft.
