- Behavior (all pure domain; no pygame, network or file I/O):
  - **Content file:**
    - `schema_version` (only 1 is accepted) and `example_only`;
    - any of the lists `ingredients`, `glasses`, `recipes` and `characters`, so the catalogue can span files.
  - **Records:**
    - **IngredientDefinition:** a category from a fixed set; ABV within 0..1; flavor tags from a controlled vocabulary.
    - **GlassDefinition:** capacity in µl.
    - **RecipeDefinition:**
      - µl quantities above 0;
      - a supported `method` (shake, stir or build);
      - steps from the supported techniques;
      - a glass, an ice style and an optional garnish;
      - `unlock_level`;
      - `variant_of`, naming the house recipe it varies.
    - **CharacterProfile:**
      - adults of 18 or older;
      - localized name and opening line;
      - preferences: liked and disliked flavors, favorite spirits and avoided ingredients.
    - **Common rules:** names need both ko and en, IDs are ASCII snake_case, and unknown fields are rejected.
  - **Strict parsing:** a quantity written as text, such as "60000", is rejected rather than converted.
  - **Checks within a recipe:**
    - an ingredient may appear only once;
    - the method must match its mixing steps (shake needs a shake step, stir a stir step, build neither);
    - a garnish ID and a garnish step go together.
  - **`build_catalogue(files)`** checks across files:
    - unique IDs (naming both files);
    - recipe ingredients exist and are not garnishes;
    - `garnish_id` is a garnish;
    - the glass exists, and the recipe's volume fits it;
    - `variant_of` resolves and points at a house recipe, not another variant;
    - characters' favorite spirits are spirits, and avoided ingredients exist;
    - `example_only` files are refused unless `allow_examples=True`.
  - **Error messages:** every problem is collected into one `ContentError`, as `file: record (id).field: message`. For example: `recipe.json: recipes[0] (daiquiri_house_example).method: Input should be 'shake', 'stir' or 'build'`.
  - **Order:**
    - a named order needs `recipe_id` and may carry adjustments (new target µl, ≥ 0; one per ingredient);
    - a recommendation has neither, but may carry revealed preferences;
    - `Order.check(catalogue, available)` rejects unknown recipe and ingredient IDs, and a named recipe outside the available set.
  - **PreparationEvent:**
    - times in ms, never negative, and the end never before the start;
    - measure events need an ingredient and an amount, and garnish events need an ingredient;
    - shake intensity within 0..1.
  - **Drink:**
    - `record()` appends events and refuses a duplicate event ID or a served/discarded drink;
    - `additions` sums measured µl per ingredient.
